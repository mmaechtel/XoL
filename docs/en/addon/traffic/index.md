# Traffic & Ground Ops

Eight plugins for traffic and ground operations. In the air, [LiveTraffic](livetraffic.md) renders real-world ADS-B traffic with TCAS integration. On the ground, [Better Pushback](betterpushback.md) replaces the default pushback with a route planner and a 3D tug, and [Follow the Greens](followthegreens.md) adds A-SMGCS taxiway guidance with green centerline lights and stop bars. [SimLoad Manager](simloadmanager.md) simulates the turnaround itself — boarding, cargo, and fuel with its own animated ground vehicles, passengers, and crew, no SGES required. Three plugins handle gates: on X-Plane 12, [openSAM](opensam.md) is the one to install — the free replacement for the SAM plugin, which since v5 also includes the docking guidance for over 5,000 Gateway airports formerly provided by [AutoDGS](autodgs.md); the standalone AutoDGS is deprecated and only relevant on X-Plane 11. [AutoGate](autogate.md) is the legacy plugin for sceneries built with the AutoGate toolkit, maintained for XP12. [XPAIS Marine Traffic](xpais_marine_traffic.md) puts live AIS ship traffic on the water — open source, but archived since July 2026.

All eight run on Linux: seven with native binaries, Follow the Greens via XPPython3 (see [Scripting](../scripting/index.md)).

- **[LiveTraffic](livetraffic.md)** — Real-time air traffic in the simulator
- **[Better Pushback](betterpushback.md)** — Realistic pushback
- **[SimLoad Manager](simloadmanager.md)** — Boarding, cargo, fuel, and ground vehicles
- **[AutoDGS](autodgs.md)** — Automatic Docking Guidance System
- **[openSAM](opensam.md)** — Jetways and airport animations
- **[AutoGate](autogate.md)** — Jetway control
- **[Follow the Greens](followthegreens.md)** — Airport taxi guidance
- **[XPAIS Marine Traffic](xpais_marine_traffic.md)** — Live AIS ship traffic (archived)
