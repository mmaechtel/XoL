# Scenery Plugins

Nine plugins and libraries that visually extend X-Plane's default world. [XRoads](xroad.md) is a scenery library rather than a plugin: it hides X-Plane's road polygons on Ortho4XP tiles so the real roads in the satellite imagery show through, while bridges, highways, and railways stay visible — with Linux compilation instructions. [Airport Enhancement Package](aep.md) upgrades default airports with better buildings, ground markings, vegetation, and PBR textures. Three plugins deal with snow: [xa-snow](xa-snow.md) was discontinued because X-Plane now draws accumulated snow itself, [NOAA Weather](noaa_weather.md) sets the snow cover from X-Plane's weather data and adds a METAR comparison, and [XP SnowState](xp_snowstate.md) — currently in beta — corrects the snow with real snow depths from measuring stations and weather models. [LST](lst.md) animates airports with vehicles, pedestrians, and ground equipment on defined routes, and [Bay's Lighting Mod](bays_lighting_mod.md) reworks airport, night, and cockpit lighting plus cloud scattering. [XP BirdState](xp_birdstate.md) adds bird flocks matched to region, season, and weather — a commercial plugin with an official Linux build — and [Night Sky Fix](night_sky_fix.md) replaces the star catalog with a denser one (Milky Way at 900,000 stars) and optionally corrects star brightness with a FlyWithLua script.

All nine run on Linux; three have requirements worth knowing before installing: Bay's Lighting Mod needs FlyWithLua, NOAA Weather needs XPPython3 (see [Scripting](../scripting/index.md)), and XP SnowState needs glibc 2.38 or newer.

- **[XRoads](xroad.md)** — Hide road polygons on ortho tiles
- **[Airport Enhancement Package](aep.md)** — Enhance airport surroundings
- **[xa-snow](xa-snow.md)** — NOAA snow depth overlay (discontinued)
- **[NOAA Weather](noaa_weather.md)** — Snow cover and METAR comparison
- **[XP SnowState](xp_snowstate.md)** — Real snow depths from stations and weather models (beta)
- **[LST](lst.md)** — Living Scenery Technology
- **[Bay's Lighting Mod](bays_lighting_mod.md)** — Complete lighting overhaul
- **[XP BirdState](xp_birdstate.md)** — Bird flocks by region, season, and weather
- **[Night Sky Fix](night_sky_fix.md)** — Denser star catalog and realistic star brightness
