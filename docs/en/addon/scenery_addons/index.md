# Scenery Plugins

Six plugins that visually extend X-Plane's default world: XRoads adds road and rail traffic, AEP enhances airport surroundings, xa-snow improves snow rendering, NOAA Weather integrates real-time weather data, LST brings scenery to life with dynamic elements, and Bay's Lighting Mod reworks the lighting system.

- **[XRoads](xroad.md)** — Road traffic and railroads
- **[Airport Enhancement Package](aep.md)** — Enhance airport surroundings
- **[xa-snow](xa-snow.md)** — Improved snow rendering
- **[NOAA Weather](noaa_weather.md)** — Real-time weather data
- **[LST](lst.md)** — Living Scenery Technology
- **[Bay's Lighting Mod](bays_lighting_mod.md)** — Complete lighting overhaul
