# Denver International Airport (KDEN) - X-Codr Designs

Denver International Airport (KDEN) is one of the largest and most modern airports in the USA, developed by X-Codr Designs in Version 2.2 for X-Plane 12. This scenery provides a detailed representation of the airport and its surroundings.

## About the Airport

Denver International Airport is the largest airport in the USA by area and the fifth busiest by passenger traffic. It serves as a major hub for United Airlines and Frontier Airlines.

**Interesting Facts:**

- The airport features six runways, with the longest (16R/34L) measuring 4,877 meters
- The characteristic tent roof of the terminal is the largest of its kind worldwide
- The airport opened in 1995, replacing the old Stapleton International Airport

## Scenery Features

The X-Codr Designs Version 2.2 offers the following main features:

- Fully modeled buildings and structures
- Detailed runway and taxiway markings
- Dynamic lighting
- Optimized performance
- HD textures
- X-Plane 12 adaptations

### Special Details

- All buildings are modeled with high detail
- Correct PAPI systems and runway lighting
- Realistic parking positions and gates
- Detailed terrain modeling

## Installation and Compatibility

The scenery supports the following systems and technologies:

- X-Plane 12
- Windows, Mac, and Linux

### System Requirements

The scenery has the following system requirements:

- Minimum 4GB VRAM recommended
- Modern CPU for optimal performance
- SSD for fast loading times

**Performance Note**: The scenery is currently quite CPU-intensive. However, updates are planned for both the scenery itself and the used libraries (such as AEP) that should improve performance.
